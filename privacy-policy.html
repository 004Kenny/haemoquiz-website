Privacy Policy

This policy explains how HaemoQuiz collects, uses, stores, and deletes information.

Information collectedHow information is usedService providersRetentionSecurityYour choicesChildrenChangesContact
HaemoQuiz is an educational application. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency medical services.

1. Information we collect

When you create and use a HaemoQuiz account, we may collect:

  • First and last name
  • Email address
  • Mobile phone number
  • Date of birth
  • Firebase user identifier
  • Quiz attempts, selected answers, scores, percentages, completion times, and quiz duration
  • App preferences stored locally on your device, such as quiz length and shuffle settings

2. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Create and secure your account
  • Load quiz content and save your results
  • Display quiz history and learning progress
  • Remember your quiz preferences
  • Respond to support and account-deletion requests
  • Maintain the safety, reliability, and performance of the service

3. Service providers

HaemoQuiz uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to authenticate users and store account and quiz data. These services process information according to their own privacy and security practices.

HaemoQuiz does not sell personal information. We do not share personal information with advertisers.

4. Data retention

Account and quiz-history data are retained while your account remains active. When you delete your account, HaemoQuiz deletes your Firebase Authentication account, profile document, and saved quiz-attempt documents, subject to any retention required by law.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including authenticated access controls and Firebase security rules. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Review your quiz history inside the app
  • Clear your quiz history from Settings
  • Change supported quiz preferences
  • Delete your account and associated data from Settings
  • Request account deletion through our public deletion page

7. Children’s privacy

HaemoQuiz is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material revisions are made.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy may be sent to support@haemoquiz.com.
