Account and data deletion

Delete your HaemoQuiz account

You can permanently delete your account and associated data from inside the app or by contacting support.

Delete in the appRequest by emailWhat is deletedWhat may be retainedProcessing time

Delete your account inside HaemoQuiz

  1. Open HaemoQuiz and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Settings.
  4. Scroll to Data & Privacy.
  5. Select Delete account.
  6. Confirm the warning and enter your password.

After successful confirmation, your account and associated HaemoQuiz data are permanently deleted.

Request deletion without access to the app

Email support@haemoquiz.com using the email address associated with your HaemoQuiz account.

Please include:

  • The subject line: HaemoQuiz Account Deletion Request
  • The email address linked to your account
  • A clear request to delete your account and associated data
For security, we may ask you to verify ownership of the account before processing deletion. Do not send your password by email.

What is deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account
  • Your HaemoQuiz profile information
  • Your saved quiz attempts and quiz history
  • User-linked preferences or records stored by HaemoQuiz, where applicable

What may be retained

HaemoQuiz does not intentionally retain active account or quiz-history data after deletion. Limited records may be retained when required by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or enforcement of legal obligations.

Processing time

In-app deletion is normally processed immediately when connectivity and authentication succeed. Email requests are reviewed and processed within 30 days after account ownership has been verified.
